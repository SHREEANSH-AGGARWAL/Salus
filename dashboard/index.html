How this dashboard works

The one-minute version

Salus tracks emergency units (ambulances, fire tenders, rescue teams) and disaster zones (places that need help) across Delhi. Either you ask the AI planner for a recommendation, or you pick a unit yourself — then you approve, change, or reject it. Only approved dispatches become real, and once a unit is committed to a zone no other command post can send it somewhere else.

Zone reported→AI planner or your own pick→ You approve→Network agrees→Unit dispatched

The approval step is never skipped — not by the AI, not by you. If the planner is not installed on this server, Send a unit manually does the same job with you choosing the unit.

The four screens

Operations
The live map, the unit and zone lists, and what needs your attention right now.
Approvals
Dispatch recommendations waiting on you. Approve, swap the unit, or reject — each with a reason. This is also where you start a dispatch, with the planner or by hand.
Network
Health of this command post and its links to the others. Mostly you only look here when something is wrong.
History
The permanent record: every AI recommendation and every human decision, in order.

Using the map

  • Drag to move around · scroll or double-click to zoom
  • Click a unit or zone to open its details on the right
  • Layers (top right) hide things you do not need — routes, place names, the sweep
  • Pick on map inside the add-unit and add-zone forms lets you click a location instead of typing coordinates
  • Rings are distance from the middle of the view; ticks are compass bearings

Words you will see

Command post / node
One Salus server. Several of them run together, one per agency control room.
Leader
The command post currently allowed to write changes. The others copy from it. If it fails, another takes over automatically.
Agreed entries
Changes a majority of command posts have stored. Once agreed, a change cannot be lost or reversed.
Majority needed
How many command posts must agree before a change counts. With 3 posts, that is 2.
Round
Increments each time the network elects a new leader. A jumping number means leadership is unstable.
Isolated / degraded
This post has lost contact with the majority. It can no longer be sure a unit is free, so every unit is shown as unconfirmed and approvals are blocked. That is deliberate.
Unconfirmed unit
We cannot currently verify this unit's status. Do not act on it.
Approval gate
The rule that no dispatch happens without a named human saying yes.
Override
You approve the dispatch but send a different unit than the one recommended.
Fallback plan
The recommendation came from simple rules instead of the AI, because the AI was unavailable. Read it more carefully.
Confidence
How sure the planner is. Below 50% treat it as a suggestion, not an answer.
